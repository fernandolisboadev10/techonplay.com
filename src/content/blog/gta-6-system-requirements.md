Your PC handled Cyberpunk 2077 on high settings. It chewed through Alan Wake 2. But **GTA 6 system requirements** are the question keeping PC gamers up at night, and the honest answer is more complicated than any spec sheet floating around right now.

Rockstar drops GTA 6 on November 19, 2026, and the hype has reached a fever pitch few games ever touch. The catch nobody talks about enough: as of today, the game is confirmed only for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. There is no PC release date, and there are no official PC specs from Rockstar. Every “confirmed” chart you’ve seen online is a guess dressed up as fact.

By the end of this guide, you’ll know exactly what’s real, what’s prediction, and how to build or upgrade a PC that’s ready the moment Rockstar finally announces the PC version.

### Quick Answer: Is There Even a GTA 6 PC Version Yet?

No. Rockstar’s official GTA 6 page lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has said publicly that a PC release is coming eventually, but not on day one, and treated that as a deliberate business call rather than a technical limitation.

History backs this up. GTA V hit PC about 18 months after its console debut. Red Dead Redemption 2 took roughly 13 months. If GTA 6 follows the same pattern, a PC launch lands sometime in 2027, with official system requirements arriving three to six months before that.

None of this means you should stop planning. It means every GTA 6 system requirements chart online right now, including ours, is an educated estimate built from console hardware, Rockstar’s engine history, and comparable AAA titles. Treat it as a planning tool, not a guarantee.

### GTA 6 System Requirements: The Three Predicted Tiers

We built these tiers from three sources: the PS5 and Xbox Series X hardware baseline, the demands of Red Dead Redemption 2 on Rockstar’s RAGE engine, and how the visual fidelity in GTA 6’s trailers compares to current top-tier releases.

#### Tier 1: Minimum (1080p, Low to Medium Settings)

This tier gets you a playable frame rate, not a pretty one. Expect texture pop-in and dropped shadows on distant objects.

-   CPU: Intel Core i5-11600K or AMD Ryzen 5 5600
-   GPU: NVIDIA RTX 3060 or AMD RX 7600
-   RAM: 16 GB
-   Storage: 150 GB, NVMe SSD required

#### Tier 2: Recommended (1440p, High Settings)

This is where most PC gamers will actually land. Solid frame rates, real ray tracing on select effects, no compromises that ruin the experience.

-   CPU: Intel Core i7-12700K or AMD Ryzen 7 5800X3D
-   GPU: NVIDIA RTX 5070 or AMD RX 9070
-   RAM: 32 GB
-   Storage: 150 GB, Gen 4 NVMe SSD

#### Tier 3: Enthusiast (1440p to 4K, Ultra with Ray Tracing)

Full ray tracing, maxed draw distances, and headroom for whatever performance mods the community throws at the game in year one.

-   CPU: AMD Ryzen 7 9800X3D or Intel Core Ultra 9 285K
-   GPU: NVIDIA RTX 5080 or better
-   RAM: 32 GB
-   Storage: 150 GB, Gen 5 NVMe SSD

### Why These Predicted Specs Make Sense

The RAGE engine has never been kind to hard drives. Red Dead Redemption 2 already leaned hard on fast storage, and GTA 6’s Leonida map is bigger. An old mechanical drive will bottleneck the asset streaming that makes the open world feel seamless, so an NVMe SSD is not optional in any tier.

The RAM jump to 32 GB for anything beyond minimum settings reflects a broader 2026 trend. Modern open-world engines lean on system memory for NPC simulation and traffic AI, both of which Rockstar has teased heavily in GTA 6 trailers.

DLSS 4 and FSR 4 support is not officially confirmed, but it would be a shock if Rockstar skipped upscaling entirely. Every major 2026 release leans on it to hit playable frame rates with ray tracing enabled, and GTA 6 will almost certainly follow.

### Does Your Current PC Stand a Chance?

You don’t need to guess. Three checks tell you almost everything.

#### Step 1: Check Your GPU

Your graphics card is the single biggest bottleneck for any Rockstar open-world game. If you’re running anything older than an RTX 3060 or RX 6600, budget for an upgrade regardless of which tier you’re targeting.

#### Step 2: Check Your RAM

Sixteen gigabytes is the new floor, not the ceiling. If you’re still at 8 GB, that upgrade should happen before you even think about the GPU.

#### Step 3: Check Your Storage

Open File Explorer or Disk Utility and confirm your main drive is an SSD, not a spinning hard drive. If GTA 6 streams the world the way RDR2 did, an HDD will cause stutter no GPU can fix.

### What to Upgrade First on a Budget

If your PC falls short, upgrade in this order for the best return per dollar.

✅ **Priority 1: NVMe SSD ($50 to $150).** Cheapest fix, biggest impact on load times and world streaming.

✅ **Priority 2: GPU ($300 to $700).** The 2026 memory chip shortage has pushed GPU prices up sharply, so shop early rather than waiting until launch week panic hits.

✅ **Priority 3: RAM, 16 GB to 32 GB ($60 to $100).** A simple swap on most modern motherboards.

❌ **Priority 4: CPU.** Only necessary if you’re still on a processor older than six years. Most modern mid-range chips will hold up fine.

### FAQ

**Is GTA 6 confirmed for PC?**

_No. Rockstar has only confirmed PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S for the November 19, 2026 launch. A PC version is expected but has no confirmed release window, based on Rockstar’s pattern with GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2._

**Can I run GTA 6 on a laptop?**

_A gaming laptop with an RTX 4060 or better, 16 GB of RAM, and an NVMe SSD should handle minimum to recommended settings once a PC version ships. Thermal throttling on thin laptops may limit sustained performance versus a desktop with the same GPU._

**Does GTA 6 support ray tracing on minimum settings?**

_Predicted minimum settings skip ray tracing entirely to protect frame rate on lower-end hardware. Ray tracing effects are expected to activate starting at the recommended tier, based on how Rockstar handled RDR2’s PC port._

**How much storage does GTA 6 need?**

_Estimates point to roughly 150 GB, in line with GTA V and RDR2’s PC footprint scaled up for Leonida’s larger map. Confirm this number once Rockstar publishes official specs._

**Should I upgrade my PC now or wait?**

_Wait on the GPU if you can, since 2026’s memory chip shortage has pushed prices up across the board. Upgrading storage and RAM now is safe and useful regardless of when GTA 6 arrives on PC._
