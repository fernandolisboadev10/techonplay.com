Google's push into smart glasses now has a price tag. The **XREAL Aura Android XR glasses** will start at $1,279 in the US, with a $1,499 version for people who want more memory and storage.

That is below the "under $1,500" figure XREAL promised earlier, but it is not a done deal. XREAL's own page says final pricing will be announced closer to launch, and the glasses are not shipping yet. [Editorial note: reservations are open, and details may change before the first deliveries.]

Below you'll find both US prices, the specs, how the reservation system works, when the first units are expected, and what to weigh before you put money down.

## How Much Do the XREAL Aura Glasses Cost?

Two configurations are listed, according to [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/10/07/xreal-aura-with-android-xr-start-at-1279/) and [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2279737/xreals-aura-android-xr-smartglasses-will-cost-you-at-least-1279/):

| 💰 Model | 🧠 Memory | 💾 Storage | 📈 US price |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🥽 Aura (base) | 12GB | 256GB | $1,279 |
| 🥽 Aura (upgrade) | 16GB | 512GB | $1,499 |

The two frame sizes cost the same. Medium fits pupil distances of 60 to 66 mm, and large covers 66 to 72 mm, so the choice depends on your face, not your budget.

Treat the numbers as provisional. XREAL's official FAQ says the base model's final retail price will not exceed $1,500 before taxes, and that final pricing and configurations arrive closer to launch. For a sense of scale, a Galaxy S26 Ultra now starts at $1,399 after Samsung's increase, as covered in our [Galaxy S26 price report](/samsung-galaxy-s26-price-increase/).

## What Is Inside the XREAL Aura?

The Aura is not a headset. It is a pair of see-through glasses connected to a separate compute puck that runs Android XR.

Here is what Engadget and XREAL list:

- 🔍 **Display:** 70-degree field of view, optical see-through lenses, Sony micro-OLED at 1920 x 1200 per eye, per XREAL.
- ⚖️ **Weight:** under 95 grams for the glasses.
- 🧠 **Chip:** Qualcomm's Snapdragon Reality Elite platform in the puck, plus XREAL's X1S coprocessor in the glasses.
- 🔊 **Audio:** Bose-engineered tuning and a four-microphone array.
- 🎮 **Controls:** hand tracking, voice, and a trackpad and fingerprint sensor on the puck.
- 🔌 **Extras:** it can plug into a computer and work as an external monitor.

Engadget's reviewer tested a version at Google's developer conference and called the visuals "extremely sharp and bright," even in sunny outdoor conditions. That is one hands-on impression, not a full review.

One detail varies by source. XREAL lists two world-facing tracking cameras for hand tracking, while 9to5Google says the glasses do not need cameras to work. Check the final spec sheet if camera privacy matters to you.

## What Can Android XR and Gemini Do on the Aura?

The puck runs Android XR with Google Play and Gemini built in. XREAL says Gemini can see what you see and that millions of Google Play apps are available from day one, including Google Maps, YouTube, Chrome and Google Photos.

The page also lists up to five virtual screens, wired mirroring for a PC display, and Bluetooth keyboard support. If you already use Google's AI assistant, our rundown of the [best Google AI tools of 2026](/best-google-ai-tools-2026/) shows what Gemini does today on phones and laptops.

How well any of this works in daily life is unproven until reviewers test final hardware.

## When Can You Buy the XREAL Aura in the US?

You can reserve now, but you can't buy a finished pair yet.

- ⏱️ **Reservations:** open now, and XREAL's page says they are refundable before you confirm a purchase.
- ⏱️ **Public orders:** open "in the coming weeks," per Engadget, or "later this month," per 9to5Google.
- ⏱️ **First deliveries:** XREAL says the first wave arrives "before the holidays."
- ⏱️ **Launch regions:** the US, Canada, Japan and South Korea first, with a wider release in 2027, according to Engadget.

9to5Google also reports a $199 priority deposit that counts toward the final price. XREAL's own page lists several reservation tiers, including a limited Founder pass capped at 2,000 reservations. The details look different across sources, so read the terms on XREAL's US store before paying anything.

## FAQ

### How much do the XREAL Aura glasses cost?

The XREAL Aura starts at $1,279 in the US for the 12GB memory and 256GB storage model. The 16GB and 512GB version costs $1,499. XREAL's FAQ says the base model's final retail price will not exceed $1,500 before taxes, and that final pricing will be confirmed closer to launch.

### When does the XREAL Aura come out?

XREAL says the first wave of deliveries arrives before the holidays, and expects a fall 2026 launch. Reservations are open now, and public orders are expected in the coming weeks. XREAL has not published an exact ship date, so treat any specific day you see elsewhere as unconfirmed.

### Do the XREAL Aura glasses work without a phone?

Yes, in the sense that they use their own compute puck running Android XR, with Google Play and Gemini built in. The puck has its own trackpad and fingerprint sensor. The glasses can also plug into a computer and act as an external monitor.

### How heavy are the XREAL Aura glasses?

The glasses weigh under 95 grams, according to Engadget and XREAL's page. That figure covers the glasses only. The compute puck is separate, and XREAL suggests wearing it on a lanyard or clipping it to clothing. Comfort over a full day has not been independently tested.

### Is the XREAL Aura available outside the US?

At launch, it is planned for the US, Canada, Japan and South Korea, with a wider release in 2027, per Engadget. XREAL's FAQ lists a longer set of supported countries, and the page does not fully reconcile the two. Prices in other countries are likely to differ, so check XREAL's local store.

The XREAL Aura Android XR glasses start at $1,279 in the US, run Android XR with Gemini, and are expected to ship before the holidays. The price is provisional, and no one has reviewed final hardware yet.
