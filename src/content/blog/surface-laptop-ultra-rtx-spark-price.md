Microsoft's most expensive Surface laptop in years is built for people who run AI models on their own machines. The **Surface Laptop Ultra** starts at $2,599 in the US, runs on NVIDIA's RTX Spark chip, and began shipping in mid-October after pre-orders opened October 7.

Microsoft says it is much faster than Apple's MacBook Pro for local AI work. Independent reviews are now out, and they are more mixed: strong graphics and memory for big models, but weaker CPU speed than a MacBook Pro. This is a roundup of those reviews, not our own hands-on test.

Below you'll find the specs, what Microsoft claims, what reviewers found, the matching $5,999 Dev Box, and who should buy or skip it.

## What Is the Surface Laptop Ultra?

According to Engadget's spec rundown, the Surface Laptop Ultra is based on NVIDIA's RTX Spark chip, sold as the RTX Spark N1X. Microsoft calls it a "builder class PC," aimed at developers and power users rather than casual buyers.

Pre-orders opened October 7, 2026, and shipping began in mid-October, per Macworld. The key hardware, per Engadget and Microsoft's product page:

- 🧠 **Chip:** NVIDIA RTX Spark with Blackwell RTX graphics and up to a 20-core NVIDIA Grace CPU.
- 💾 **Memory:** up to 128GB of unified memory shared by the CPU and GPU.
- 📦 **Storage:** up to 2TB, and user-upgradable.
- 🖥️ **Display:** 15-inch mini-LED touchscreen at 120 Hz with up to 2,000 nits of peak HDR brightness.
- ⚖️ **Build:** aluminum, under 4.5 pounds and under 18 mm thick, in Platinum and Nightfall.

The 15-inch size comes from Microsoft. Engadget's hands-on describes a 16-inch laptop, which looks like an error, so trust Microsoft's spec sheet.

## How Much Does the Surface Laptop Ultra Cost in the US?

Pricing starts at $2,599 (Microsoft's store lists $2,599.99). Prices climb with memory. PCWorld lists the top configuration at $5,899.99 and its 32GB review unit at $3,699.99. Earlier reports disagreed on the base RAM, so check Microsoft's configurator before you order.

| 💰 Item | 📊 Price and details |
|---|---|
| 💻 Surface Laptop Ultra | From $2,599 |
| 🖥️ Surface RTX Spark Dev Box | From $5,999, ships in November |
| ♻️ Trade-in offer | Up to $1,000 off at the Microsoft Store, October 7 to November 23 (US and Canada) |

Microsoft's page does not clearly state whether a charger is included: a footnote says only specific configurations in select markets ship with the 140W Surface Power Supply. Trade-in terms say all trade-ins are final.

## How Fast Is It? Microsoft's Claims vs Reality

Microsoft compared the Surface Laptop Ultra with a MacBook Pro using the M5 Pro chip. According to Engadget, Microsoft claims:

| ⚡ Task | 📈 Claimed advantage |
|---|---|
| 🎬 Video generation | 6.2x faster |
| 🖼️ Image creation | 4.3x faster |
| 💬 Time to first token | 2.1x faster |

Microsoft also says the chip delivers up to one petaflop of FP4 AI compute and keeps 99% of its peak performance on battery, and that cooling has 2.5 times the thermal capacity of its previous notebooks. On its product page, Microsoft says the machine supports CUDA and local AI models up to 120 billion parameters.

These are vendor claims on selected tasks. The reviews below test different things.

## What Do the Reviews Say?

Reviews published after launch point the same way: a very good laptop for a narrow group of buyers.

- ⚡ **CPU speed:** Macworld found the chip's multi-core score about 21% slower than the M5 Pro in Geekbench 7, with single-core also behind Apple. Macworld concludes Apple has nothing to worry about for general productivity.
- 🎮 **Graphics and local AI:** PCWorld says GPU performance is strong, landing near a top-tier gaming laptop in the ComfyUI image benchmark. Its verdict is that the high price only makes sense for buyers who need that AI capability, and memory matters most.
- 🔋 **Battery:** PCWorld measured about 10 hours on an office-work benchmark. Macworld's own 4K local-video test ran far longer, around 20 hours, but the two methods differ. Microsoft's own figure is up to 15 hours of local video on a preproduction unit.
- 🧩 **Compatibility:** PCWorld needed a beta version of Adobe Premiere to run it, which it ties to Windows on Arm and GPU gaps.
- 💵 **Price against Apple:** Macworld compares the $3,699 review configuration (32GB, 1TB) with a 16-inch M5 Pro MacBook Pro with 48GB and 1TB at $3,599.

Reviewers tested different configurations and power modes, so the numbers are not directly comparable.

### The Engadget hands-on

Devindra Hardawar's hands-on at Engadget, written before launch, is positive on build quality. He calls the keyboard tactile, the haptic touchpad large and responsive, and the screen "surprisingly bright." He writes that it "feels better than any other Windows laptop I've held before."

On performance, his one test was a game: Gears of War: E-Day ran smoothly at slightly above 1080p, both plugged in and on battery. He had not yet seen it run heavy AI workloads.

He also adds a caution. He calls it "just a premium laptop" and notes that RTX Spark's benefits are "meant for developers and power users," unlike Apple Silicon, which helped everyday Mac buyers right away. The full reviews above back that up.

## What Is the $5,999 Surface RTX Spark Dev Box?

Microsoft also opened pre-orders for a desktop version. Per Engadget, the Surface RTX Spark Dev Box costs about $6,000 and ships in November.

- 🧠 **Memory and storage:** 128GB of unified memory and 2TB of removable storage.
- 🪟 **Software:** Windows 11 Pro with Visual Studio Code, Git, GitHub CLI, GitHub Copilot and Python preinstalled.
- 🤖 **Claim:** it can run AI models "exceeding 120B parameters."
- 🔌 **Ports:** two USB-C, one USB-A, Ethernet, DisplayPort 2.1 and HDMI 2.1b.

Engadget describes it as a larger, pricier take on NVIDIA's DGX Spark, and expects the price to be a major obstacle for anyone but enterprises. For desktop AI builds on a normal budget, see our guide to the [best gaming PCs for AI and gaming](/best-gaming-pc-ai-and-gaming-2026/).

## What Should You Do Before Buying?

The laptop is shipping, so the main risk is paying for memory or speed you won't use.

- ✅ **Read the full reviews.** Sustained noise and power draw under long AI loads deserve a close look; PCWorld flags fan-noise concerns.
- ✅ **Compare configurations.** Memory drives the price, so decide how large a model you really need.
- ✅ **Use the trade-in offer if you qualify.** Microsoft's offer runs through November 23.
- ✅ **Check driver support.** Any new GPU platform can have teething problems, so see our guide on [fixing NVIDIA driver crashes](/fix-nvidia-driver-crashing-2026/).
- ❌ **Don't order from unofficial listings.** Use Microsoft or a major US retailer.
- ❌ **Don't buy on Microsoft's speed charts alone.** Independent CPU tests are less flattering.

## FAQ

### How much does the Surface Laptop Ultra cost?

The Surface Laptop Ultra starts at $2,599 in the US, and Microsoft's store lists $2,599.99. Pre-orders opened October 7, 2026. Prices rise with memory, up to 128GB: PCWorld lists a $3,699.99 32GB review unit and a $5,899.99 top configuration. Confirm in Microsoft's configurator before ordering.

### What chip does the Surface Laptop Ultra use?

It uses NVIDIA's RTX Spark platform, sold as the RTX Spark N1X, which combines Blackwell RTX graphics with up to a 20-core NVIDIA Grace CPU and up to 128GB of unified memory. Microsoft says it supports CUDA and local AI models up to 120 billion parameters.

### Is the Surface Laptop Ultra faster than a MacBook Pro?

Microsoft says so for specific AI tasks, claiming 6.2x faster video generation, 4.3x faster image creation and 2.1x faster time to first token versus a MacBook Pro with M5 Pro. These are Microsoft's own figures. Independent reviews found strong GPU results but a CPU slower than the M5 Pro, and Macworld did not run the AI tests.

### When does the Surface Laptop Ultra ship?

Macworld reports the laptop has started shipping, with launch-week reviews already published. Microsoft's product page lists no ship date, and press reports put it at October 16. The related Surface RTX Spark Dev Box ships in November, per Engadget.

### Is the Surface Laptop Ultra good for gaming?

Graphics are its strongest point. Engadget's reviewer ran Gears of War: E-Day smoothly at slightly above 1080p, plugged in and on battery, and PCWorld measured about 130 watts of draw while gaming. Microsoft lists ray tracing, DLSS and Reflex on its 120 Hz display. Dedicated gaming laptops may still be faster per dollar.

## The Bottom Line

The Surface Laptop Ultra is a $2,599 bet on local AI: a lighter 15-inch laptop with NVIDIA's RTX Spark chip, up to 128GB of memory, and a speed pitch aimed squarely at the MacBook Pro. Microsoft's speed claims are for AI tasks, and reviewers confirm strong graphics and big memory but find the CPU behind a comparable MacBook Pro.

If you need to run large models locally, it is worth a serious look. For general productivity, a MacBook Pro or a cheaper Windows laptop is the better buy.
