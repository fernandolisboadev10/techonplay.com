Sony is giving the standard PlayStation 5 a feature that, until now, belonged to the PS5 Pro. **PS5 QSSR**, short for Quick Spectral Super Resolution, is a new AI upscaler that sharpens image detail and steadies the picture on the base console.

Sony announced it on October 1 on the PlayStation Blog, and two games already have patches live. The big question for most players is simple: does your PS5 get a visual upgrade, and what does it cost you? Short answer: it's free, but only some games support it so far.

Below you'll see what QSSR is, how it compares with PSSR on the PS5 Pro, which games support it, and how to turn it on.

## What Is PS5 QSSR?

QSSR is a performance tier of AI upscaling. According to [the PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/10/01/ai-upscaling-is-coming-to-ps5/), it analyzes game images pixel by pixel and uses AI to improve detail and stability.

It comes out of Project Amethyst, an AI graphics collaboration between Sony Interactive Entertainment and AMD. Engadget reports Sony built QSSR on "a streamlined neural network architecture" and "a hand-tuned implementation that maximizes performance" on the PS5.

The "quick" part matters. QSSR asks less of the console than PSSR does. That is how Sony can run AI upscaling on hardware that is less powerful than the PS5 Pro.

## PS5 QSSR vs PSSR: What's the Difference?

PSSR is the AI upscaler that has been on the PS5 Pro for two years. Sony still calls it "the gold standard on PS5 Pro." QSSR is the lighter version for the standard PS5.

| 🎮 Feature | 🧠 PSSR | ⚡ QSSR |
|---|---|---|
| 🖥️ Console | PS5 Pro only | Standard PS5 |
| 🏷️ Name | PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution | Quick Spectral Super Resolution |
| ⚙️ Computing demand | Higher | Lower than PSSR |
| 🏆 Sony's positioning | "The gold standard on PS5 Pro" | Notable gains in detail and stability on PS5 |

One caveat: Sony has not published frame rates, resolution targets, or how much GPU time QSSR uses. Don't expect a precise numbers-based comparison until independent testers get hands-on time.

## Which Games Support PS5 QSSR Right Now?

Two games, both patched on announcement day:

- ✅ **Marvel's Wolverine**
- ✅ **Ghost of Yōtei**

Insomniac Games' Mike Fitzgerald said QSSR "brings a great taste of the PS5 Pro experience to our players on PS5." Mike Fitzgerald also told Engadget it adds "more pixel-level clarity and stability to our environments, bringing out extra detail amidst the busy streets of Madripoor."

Sucker Punch's Jasmin Patry went further on stability, saying QSSR achieves "temporal stability that hasn't been possible on the PS5 console until now."

Sony says QSSR "will be offered broadly to all PlayStation developers," so more games should follow. No list or dates have been published. Engadget also notes that the Ghost of Yōtei Complete Edition releases the same day, with a $15 upgrade option for existing owners.

## How to Turn On QSSR on Your PS5

Sony says supported games get a patch that adds QSSR as "an additional graphics option on PS5." Here's the practical path:

1. 🔄 **Update the game.** Download the latest patch for Marvel's Wolverine or Ghost of Yōtei.
2. ⚙️ **Open the game's graphics settings.** Look for the new QSSR option.
3. 🎯 **Pick your mode.** Compare the result with your usual setting, because the sharper look may trade against other options.
4. 🔁 **Test in a busy scene.** Stability and fine detail show up best in crowded environments.

I'm describing the menu from Sony's wording. The exact label and layout can differ per game, and Sony has not published a step-by-step guide.

## What Do We Still Not Know About QSSR?

Quite a lot, and it matters before you rearrange your setup:

- ❓ **Performance cost:** no published frame rates or resolution numbers.
- ❓ **Game list:** only two titles so far, with no roadmap.
- ❓ **Third-party adoption:** developers can add it, but no studio beyond Sony's own has been named.
- ❓ **How close it gets to PSSR:** Sony says PSSR remains the best on PS5 Pro, and has not measured the gap publicly.

If you own a PS5 Pro, nothing changes for you. PSSR stays your upscaler.

## FAQ

### What does QSSR stand for on PS5?

QSSR stands for Quick Spectral Super Resolution. It is a performance tier of AI upscaling that Sony announced for the standard PlayStation 5 on October 1, 2026. It analyzes game images pixel by pixel to improve detail and stability, and it came out of Sony's Project Amethyst collaboration with AMD.

### Is QSSR the same as PSSR?

No. PSSR (PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution) runs on the PS5 Pro, and Sony calls it the gold standard there. QSSR is a lighter, faster version for the standard PS5. It uses a streamlined neural network and a hand-tuned PS5 implementation that makes fewer demands on the console.

### Which PS5 games support QSSR?

At launch, Marvel's Wolverine and Ghost of Yōtei. Both were patched on October 1, 2026, with QSSR as an extra graphics option. Sony says every PlayStation developer will be able to add it, but it has not announced more games or release dates.

### Do I need a PS5 Pro to use QSSR?

No. QSSR is made for the standard PS5. The PS5 Pro keeps PSSR, which Sony says remains the gold standard on that console. You only need the latest game patch and a game that supports the feature.

### Does QSSR cost anything?

Sony has not announced a charge for QSSR itself, and it arrives as a patch to supported games. One related cost: the Ghost of Yōtei Complete Edition has a $15 upgrade option for existing owners, according to Engadget. That upgrade is separate from the QSSR option.

## The Bottom Line

PS5 QSSR is a real step for standard PS5 owners: AI upscaling that improves image detail and stability, free through game patches, starting with Marvel's Wolverine and Ghost of Yōtei. The open questions are performance numbers and how fast other studios adopt it.
