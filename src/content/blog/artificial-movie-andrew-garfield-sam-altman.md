## Who Plays Who in the Artificial Movie Cast

Casting a tech drama is tricky. Get it wrong and the whole thing reads like a parody. Neon went with a lineup built for award-season buzz, not stunt casting.

Simon Rich wrote the script, which explains the trailer’s dark comedy undertone. This is not a dry corporate documentary. It is being pitched as a workplace thriller with jokes.

Barbaro’s take on Murati leans into the impossible position she was put in: running a company for five days while its future was decided by people who would not explain themselves.

## The Real Story Behind the Artificial Movie

If you were not glued to tech Twitter in November 2023, here is the short version. If the acronym in the story is new to you, our explainer on [what GPT stands for](/what-does-gpt-stand-for/) covers it in plain English, and our guide on [how to use ChatGPT effectively](/how-to-use-chatgpt-effectively/) shows what the product does today.

OpenAI’s board fired Sam Altman without warning, citing a breakdown in trust. No specific misconduct was ever detailed publicly. Within 48 hours, more than 700 of OpenAI’s roughly 770 employees signed a letter threatening to quit unless the board resigned and reinstated Altman.

Microsoft, which had invested billions into OpenAI, publicly offered Altman and his team jobs on the spot. Five days later, the board reversed course. Altman came back as CEO, and most of the board members who voted him out were gone within weeks.

It remains one of the strangest corporate stories of the AI boom: a company nearly imploded over a disagreement that its own board still has not fully explained.

That silence is exactly why a scripted retelling works. A documentary needs cooperation from its subjects. A drama does not. Guadagnino and Rich are free to imagine the closed-door conversations that Altman, Sutskever, and the rest of the board have never fully addressed in public, which is likely where the film’s dark comedy angle will do the most work.
