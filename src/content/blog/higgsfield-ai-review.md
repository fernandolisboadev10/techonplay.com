If you typed **Higgsfield AI** into Google, you probably want three answers fast: is it free, how much does it cost, and is it worth paying for. Short version: the free plan gives you no video, the paid plans start at $19 a month, and the real price depends on how many credits each clip burns.

Higgsfield went from a niche creator tool to one of the most talked-about AI video apps of the year. In August 2026 it announced a $400 million round at a $5.4 billion valuation. That money buys attention, but it does not tell you what a 10-second clip costs you. This guide does.

\[Editorial note: we did not run a hands-on test for this article. Prices, credits and limits come from Higgsfield's pricing data as captured by third-party reviews between August 31 and October 23, 2026. Higgsfield changes plans often, so check the checkout page before you pay.\]

## What Higgsfield AI Is (and Who Is Behind It)

Higgsfield is an all-in-one AI studio for short videos and images that runs mostly in your browser. You type a prompt or upload a photo, pick a model, and it returns a clip or still. Its pitch is cinematic control: camera moves, lighting, character consistency and effects built into the interface instead of buried in prompts.

Two things set it apart from a single-model app:

- **It is a hub.** Besides its own models (Soul for images and DoP for video), it offers third-party models such as Kling, Seedance, Veo, Wan and Google's Nano Banana for images. One login, many engines.
- **It is built around workflows.** Cinema Studio handles camera and scene control, and other tools cover face swap, lip sync, storyboards and ad-style videos.

The company, Higgsfield Inc., is based in San Francisco and was co-founded by Alex Mashrabov (CEO) and Yerzat Dulat. Mashrabov previously co-founded AI Factory, which Snap bought in 2019.

## Is Higgsfield AI Free?

Technically yes, practically no. According to the pricing snapshot Krea published in October, the Free plan includes:

- 0 monthly credits
- 1 generation at a time
- Image generation on two models only
- No video
- Marked "limited use," and not for commercial work

So if you want to make a video, you need a paid plan. The free tier is a way to look around the interface, not a way to make content. Some reviews also mention a small one-time paid access pass with a few dozen credits, but sources disagree on whether it is still offered, so do not count on it.

## Higgsfield AI Pricing in 2026

Here are the paid plans as listed in the August 31 pricing snapshot, excluding taxes:

| Plan | Monthly | Billed annually | Credits per month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | 0 |
| Starter | $19 | $19 (no discount) | 270 |
| Plus | $59 | $47 a month | 1,200 |
| Ultra | $129 | $99 a month | 3,000 |

Three details matter more than the sticker price:

1. **Credits do not roll over.** Unused subscription credits disappear when the plan renews. Top-up packs expire after 90 days.
2. **Starter is the stripped-down tier.** Per Krea's reading of the pricing page, it leaves out the newest premium models, including Seedance 2.5 and Veo 3.1. If those are why you came, you are looking at Plus or Ultra.
3. **"Unlimited" has limits.** Unlimited windows apply to selected models on the website only, and they can run in a slower queue at busy times.

## What a Clip Really Costs in Credits

This is where the value question gets answered. Each generation spends credits based on model, resolution and length. Using the credit guide numbers Krea quoted for video:

| Model | 5 sec, 720p | 5 sec, 1080p | 10 sec, 720p | 10 sec, 1080p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wan 2.7 | 8 | 13 | 15 | 25 |
| Kling 3.0 | 10 | 12.5 | 20 | 25 |
| Seedance 2.0 | 23 | 45 | 45 | 90 |
| Cinema Studio | 25 | 50 | 50 | 100 |

Do the math on Starter's 270 credits. At Kling 3.0 prices, that is roughly 27 five-second clips at 720p. Switch to Cinema Studio at 1080p and it is about five. Re-rolls and upscales cost extra credits, and most people regenerate a clip several times before keeping one.

One independent review, from Hack'celeration, put it in dollars: its tester finished 15 videos at roughly $2 each, on a plan tier that no longer matches today's price list, and rated Higgsfield 2.8 out of 5 for value. Its main complaints were that credit costs are hard to see before you click generate and that support was email-only, with replies in 36 to 48 hours.

## What Higgsfield Does Well

The same review scored the features and depth at 4.2 out of 5. The strengths people keep mentioning:

- **Camera control.** Cinema Studio offers pan, tilt, dolly and tracking moves without writing a film-school prompt. If you want to learn those terms anyway, our [Google Flow camera commands guide](/google-flow-camera-commands/) shows 40 you can reuse.
- **Many models in one place.** You can test the same idea on different engines and keep the best result.
- **Fast first result.** The reviewer went from sign-up to a first video in about five minutes.
- **One-click upscale and lip sync.** Both were easy to use in testing.

## The Catches to Know Before You Subscribe

Read these before you enter a card number:

- **Credit shock.** A single premium-model render with sound or an upscale pass can eat 70 to 85 credits.
- **Commercial use is unclear.** The pricing table says the Free plan is not for commercial use, while Krea notes the Terms of Use do not restrict commercial use of outputs. Krea suggests asking support which document applies if you sell what you make.
- **Training license.** According to Krea's reading of the Terms, Higgsfield may use your inputs and outputs to train its models unless you delete the content or have an Enterprise agreement. Do not upload private photos you would not want reused.
- **Web first.** Higgsfield is built for the browser. One guide says its phone app is called Diffuse, while another review found no official mobile app, so confirm in your app store.
- **Conflicting prices online.** Many pages still show old plans. Trust the checkout screen.

One more caution: Krea, which wrote two of the pricing breakdowns used here, sells a competing product. We used it for the pricing data, which matches other sources, and not for quality opinions.

## How to Get the Most From Your Credits

Spend credits like a budget, not a slot machine:

1. **Draft cheap, finish expensive.** Test the idea on a low-cost model at 720p, then render the winner on a premium model at 1080p.
2. **Start from a good still.** Generate or upload a strong first frame, then animate it. It wastes fewer credits than hoping text alone lands.
3. **Keep clips short.** A 5-second clip costs about half of a 10-second one. Stitch short shots in an editor.
4. **Cancel on purpose.** Credits reset every cycle, so use them before renewal.

## FAQ

### Is Higgsfield AI free to use?

Not for video. The Free plan has 0 credits, one generation at a time and image generation on two models. Paid plans start at $19 a month.

### How much does Higgsfield AI cost per month?

Starter is $19 for 270 credits, Plus is $59 monthly or $47 billed annually for 1,200 credits, and Ultra is $129 monthly or $99 billed annually for 3,000 credits, excluding taxes.

### Do Higgsfield credits roll over?

No. Subscription credits reset at renewal. Purchased packs expire 90 days after purchase.

### Is Higgsfield AI legit?

It is a real company with major backing. Reuters reported a $400 million Series B at a $5.4 billion valuation in August 2026, led by DST. Reviews are mixed on value, mostly because of credit costs.

### Can I use Higgsfield videos commercially?

Paid plans appear to allow it, but sources disagree on which document governs. Confirm with Higgsfield support in writing before selling client work.

## Conclusion

Higgsfield is powerful, but it rewards planning. The free plan is a demo, Starter runs out quickly on premium models, and credits vanish each month.
