OpenAI just handed Free users a real scheduler. On August 25, ChatGPT began letting Free accounts create up to three active scheduled tasks, so **ChatGPT free automations** no longer require a paid plan.

The feature launched on June 17 as a paid perk. About ten weeks later, OpenAI opened a limited version to everyone: tasks that run once or repeat no more than once per day. Earlier in August, Free and Go users also got unlimited text chats (with abuse guardrails) and a Think button for harder questions.

This guide shows what the Free plan can and cannot schedule, how to set up your first task in about two minutes, and which five tasks deserve your three slots. It also covers copy-paste prompts for everything the scheduler skips, plus free Zapier and Make backups.

## What ChatGPT Free Automations Can Do in September 2026

Here is the short version of what Free accounts get, based on OpenAI’s help center and release notes:

✅ Up to **3** active scheduled tasks (the Go plan has the same cap) ✅ One-time or recurring runs, no more than once per day, inside flexible scheduling windows ✅ Results by push notification, email, or both ✅ Task sharing with other people ❌ Hourly schedules (paid plans only) ❌ Triggers from Gmail, Slack, or GitHub (Free and Go cannot create them) ❌ Voice chats or custom GPTs inside a task ❌ ChatGPT agent mode (paid plans only)

Think of a scheduled task as a saved prompt with an alarm clock. ChatGPT runs it at the time you set and sends you the result. It tells you things. It does not click buttons, reply to emails, or file documents for you.

## How to Set Up Your First Scheduled Task on ChatGPT Free

You do not need special settings. You need one clear instruction and a time.

### Step 1: Open Scheduled and describe the job

Open the Scheduled section in ChatGPT on web or mobile. Then write the job in plain language, the way you would brief a human assistant: what to check, when, and what the answer should look like.

### Step 2: Pick a daily window

Free tasks run at most once per day, and OpenAI describes the timing as flexible windows rather than exact minutes. Coverage of the update reports choices like morning, afternoon, and night, and says exact delivery times are a paid feature.

### Step 3: Choose how ChatGPT reaches you

Go to Settings, then Notifications, and select push, email, or both. Email suits a daily briefing you read on a laptop. Push suits alerts you cannot miss.

### Step 4: Test the prompt before you schedule it

Run the prompt in a normal chat first. When the answer looks right, paste it into a task. A vague prompt wastes one of your three slots.

## 5 Scheduled Tasks Worth Your Three Free Slots

Pick tasks that repay you every day or every week. Each prompt below fits the once-per-day limit. Replace the brackets with your own details.

### 1\. The Morning Briefing

> Every weekday morning, give me the five most important news stories in \[industry\]. Write one sentence per story on why it matters to \[my role\]. Skip anything older than 24 hours.

### 2\. The Price or Change Watch

> Check once a day whether \[product or page\] has dropped below \[$X\] or changed its \[policy\]. Message me only when something changed. Include the source.

### 3\. The Friday Review

> Every Friday afternoon, ask me five short questions about my week. Then turn my answers into a summary with wins, blockers, and three priorities for Monday.

### 4\. The Daily Practice Drill

> Each evening, send me one practice question on \[topic\] at \[level\]. Wait for my answer, then grade it and explain the gap.

### 5\. The Deadline Nudge

> Every day, list what I need to submit in the next seven days from this list: \[paste deadlines\]. Put the closest date first and flag anything due in under 48 hours.

If you can keep only three, start with the briefing, the watch, and the Friday review. Rotate the drill and the nudge in when a deadline or exam gets close.

## Copy-Paste Prompt Automations for Everything Tasks Can’t Do

Scheduled tasks skip voice, files, and GPTs. For those jobs, a reusable prompt still saves hours. Start with a master template so ChatGPT stops guessing. Prompt quality decides whether a task works, so skim [how to use ChatGPT effectively](/how-to-use-chatgpt-effectively/) first.

```
You are [role, e.g., senior project manager].
Context: [paste email, notes, or scenario].
Goal: [specific outcome].
Constraints: [tone, word count, what to avoid].
Output format: [table, bullets, or email draft].
If you need more information, ask me up to 3 questions first.
```

Then plug in one of these three workflows:

⏱️ **Meeting minutes:** “Turn these notes into minutes with decisions, action items (owner and date), and open questions. Keep it under 200 words. \[paste notes\]” ⏱️ **Email triage:** “Reply to this email in three versions: short, neutral, and warm. Keep each under 80 words. \[paste email\]” ⏱️ **SOP builder:** “Turn this rambling explanation into a numbered SOP with prerequisites, steps, and a final checklist. \[paste explanation\]”

## When Free Runs Out: Zapier and Make Free Plans

ChatGPT on the Free plan cannot move data between apps. Zapier and Make can, and both offer free tiers. Spreadsheet users can also automate work with [Copilot in Excel](/copilot-excel-hacks/).

✅ **Zapier Free:** 100 tasks per month, unlimited Zaps, two-step Zaps (one trigger and one action), and a 15-minute polling interval. Free versions of Zapier Chatbots and Agents are included. ✅ **Make Free:** 1,000 credits per month, 2 active scenarios, a 15-minute minimum interval, and 3,000+ app integrations. ❌ Both cap volume. A busy inbox can burn through 100 Zapier tasks fast.

A practical split: let ChatGPT do the thinking and let Zapier or Make do the moving. Vendors change free plans often, so check the pricing pages before you build.

## Limits and Risks to Know Before You Build

### Custom GPTs are on the way out

OpenAI plans to retire custom GPTs on December 11, 2026, across all plans. It is migrating them to plugins that bundle instructions, reference files, and connected apps. Scheduled tasks do not support GPTs anyway, so build your automations on plain prompts.

### Share links expose your instructions

Anyone with a shared task link can read its full instructions. Keep passwords, account numbers, and health details out of task titles and prompts.

### Some Free caps stay hidden

A September 16 usage tracker notes that OpenAI does not publish daily numbers for several Free tools, including image generation and Think. Watch the in-app messages to learn when you hit a cap.

_Yes. Since August 25, 2026, Free users can create up to three active scheduled tasks. Each task runs once or repeats no more than once per day, inside flexible scheduling windows. Hourly schedules and triggers from Gmail, Slack, or GitHub stay on paid plans._

### How many scheduled tasks does each ChatGPT plan allow?

_Free and Go allow three active tasks. Plus allows five, Business and Edu allow ten, and Pro and Enterprise allow fifteen. If you hit your cap on the Free plan, remove a task you no longer need to open a slot for a new one._

### How do I get the results of a scheduled task?

_Open Settings, then Notifications, and choose push, email, or both. ChatGPT sends each result through the channel you pick, so you do not need to keep the app open. Results also appear in the Scheduled section, where you can review or edit the task._

### Can ChatGPT free automations replace Zapier?

_No, because they solve different problems. Scheduled tasks generate summaries, reminders, and alerts. Zapier’s free plan moves data between apps, with 100 tasks per month and two-step Zaps. Use ChatGPT for thinking and Zapier or Make for connecting your apps._
