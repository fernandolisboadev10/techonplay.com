Samsung just made its flagship phones more expensive. The **Samsung Galaxy S26 price increase** took effect October 1 in the US: most models cost $100 more, and the top 1TB Ultra jumped $200.

The change landed with no warning, and it hit the whole S26 lineup at once. Samsung's own US website shows the new prices, while at least some Amazon listings still showed the old ones when outlets checked. [Editorial note: retailer prices may change quickly, so confirm the price at checkout.]

Here is every new price, why Samsung says it happened, which phones were spared, and what you can do before you buy.

## New Galaxy S26 Prices in the US

According to [Android Authority's price breakdown](https://www.androidauthority.com/samsung-galaxy-s26-series-price-increase-3717926/), the S26, S26+, and S26 Ultra all went up in every storage tier. Here is the full list:

| 📱 Model | 💾 Storage | 💰 Old price | 📈 New price | ➕ Increase |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Galaxy S26 | 256GB | $899.99 | $999.99 | $100 |
| Galaxy S26 | 512GB | $1,099.99 | $1,199.99 | $100 |
| Galaxy S26+ | 256GB | $1,099.99 | $1,199.99 | $100 |
| Galaxy S26+ | 512GB | $1,299.99 | $1,399.99 | $100 |
| Galaxy S26 Ultra | 256GB | $1,299.99 | $1,399.99 | $100 |
| Galaxy S26 Ultra | 512GB | $1,499.99 | $1,599.99 | $100 |
| Galaxy S26 Ultra | 1TB | $1,799.99 | $1,999.99 | $200 |

One thing to flag: Engadget lists the base S26 at a different starting point, so check Samsung.com for the exact figure on your configuration. The $100 jump is consistent across sources.

The cheapest way into the lineup now costs just under $1,000. The 1TB Ultra is a cent short of $2,000.

## Why Did Samsung Raise Galaxy S26 Prices?

Samsung's explanation, as reported by Android Authority, is short: "The global shortage of memory components has widely impacted the tech industry."

Engadget adds context. It reports that Samsung's mobile division blamed higher costs for components like RAM and storage after posting its first loss in the April to June 2026 quarter. Engadget said it contacted Samsung for clarification and had no response when it published. Treat the loss detail as Engadget's reporting, not a Samsung quote.

Memory is the part to watch. Phones with more storage got the biggest hit, and the 1TB Ultra is the only model that rose by $200.

## Which Samsung Phones Did Not Get a Price Increase?

Two lines were left alone, according to [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/10/01/samsung-galaxy-s26-price-hike/):

- ✅ **Galaxy S26 FE:** no change.
- ✅ **Foldables, including the Galaxy Z Fold 8:** no change.

That leaves the FE as the cheapest S26-family option that avoids the hike. Foldables are also still discounted. 9to5Google's deal roundup lists the Galaxy Z Fold 8 at up to $546 off at some retailers on October 1.

## Is the Increase Happening Outside the US?

Yes, in at least one other market. Android Authority reports the hike applies in the US and South Korea. In South Korea, the entry-level S26 with 256GB went from 1,254,000 won (about $919) to 1,403,600 won (about $1,029).

The headline of 9to5Google's story notes the increase "hasn't changed everywhere," so a global rollout is not confirmed. If you're buying abroad, check your local Samsung store.

The new prices are live on Samsung.com. These steps can help you avoid paying the full amount: Hardware prices are a theme this fall. See the [Steam Frame](/steam-frame-repairability/) headset's price and repairability, or our [iPhone Duo review](/iphone-duo-review/).

- ✅ **Look for carrier deals.** Verizon, T-Mobile, and AT&T often discount phones with new lines or trade-ins. Compare the final cost after bill credits, not the sticker price.
- ✅ **Consider the S26 FE or a Z Fold 8 deal** if you want a Samsung flagship without the hike.

## FAQ

### How much did Samsung raise Galaxy S26 prices?

Samsung raised most Galaxy S26, S26+, and S26 Ultra prices by $100 in the US on October 1, 2026. The 1TB Galaxy S26 Ultra rose by $200. The base Galaxy S26 with 256GB now starts at $999.99, the S26+ at $1,199.99, and the Ultra at $1,399.99.

### Why did Samsung raise the price of the Galaxy S26?

Samsung pointed to a global shortage of memory components that has affected the tech industry. Engadget reports the mobile division cited higher RAM and storage costs after posting its first loss in the April to June 2026 quarter. Samsung had not answered Engadget's request for comment at publication.

### Did the Galaxy S26 FE or Galaxy Z Fold 8 price go up?

No. According to 9to5Google, the Galaxy S26 FE and Samsung's foldables, including the Galaxy Z Fold 8, were not part of the price increase. Retailers were also running Z Fold 8 deals of up to $546 off on October 1, so foldables may be the better value right now.

### Are Amazon and Best Buy charging the new price?

Not always. Android Authority reported that some Amazon listings still showed the original prices after Samsung.com updated. Android Authority found no information on Best Buy or carrier pricing, so check the retailer's current listing. Prices at third-party stores can change at any time.

### Will the Galaxy S26 price go back down?

Nothing in the reports says so. Samsung tied the increase to memory costs, and it has not announced a timeline to reverse it. Retailer and carrier promotions are the most likely way to pay less, so watch for deals before holiday shopping events.

## The Bottom Line

The Samsung Galaxy S26 price increase adds $100 to nearly every model, with a $200 jump on the 1TB Ultra, and the cause is a memory shortage that is hitting the whole industry. The FE and foldables are exempt for now.

If you need a Galaxy S26, compare Samsung.com, Amazon, and your carrier before you pay, and look at the FE or a Z Fold 8 deal as alternatives. We'll update this page if Samsung or retailers change their pricing.
