Apple's next smart home push may arrive with someone else's name on the box. According to a new report, **Apple and LG smart home devices** are on the way: a smart lock, a doorbell, a thermostat and security cameras, all reportedly sold under the LG brand.

The story comes from Bloomberg, which says the products were co-developed with Apple. Neither company has confirmed it. [Editorial note: this is a rumor, and the details may change after Apple's expected announcement next week.]

Below you'll find what the report says, what is still unknown, how it fits with Apple's rumored home hub, and what to watch if you're shopping for smart home gear in the US.

## What Is Reportedly Coming From Apple and LG?

Mark Gurman reported the partnership at Bloomberg on October 6, 2026. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/06/apple-is-reportedly-partnering-with-lg-to-launch-a-smart-lock-thermostat-and-doorbell/) and [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2279541/apple-reportedly-worked-with-lg-on-gadgets-for-its-upcoming-smart-home-push/) both summarize it. The reported lineup:

- 🔒 **Smart deadbolt lock**
- 🔔 **Video doorbell**
- 🌡️ **Smart thermostat**
- 📷 **Indoor and outdoor security cameras**
- 💡 **Floodlight camera**

Engadget says Bloomberg's report cites LG regulatory filings. Those filings are the reason the story has legs: in the US, a wireless product generally needs Federal Communications Commission authorization before it can be sold, and the paperwork often reveals products early.

One detail stands out. The devices will reportedly carry the **LG brand**, with LG handling both manufacturing and customer support, rather than carrying an Apple logo.

## When Will the Apple and LG Smart Home Devices Launch?

Reports point to an announcement as soon as next week, but a store date is a different story.

| ⏱️ Item | 📅 What's reported |
|---|---|
| 📣 Announcement | "Next week," with an expected date of October 13, 2026 |
| 🏠 Home hub | Reportedly launching around the same time |
| 🛒 LG accessories on shelves | Not for "at least a few more months," per Engadget |
| 💰 US pricing | Not reported |

In other words, even if Apple shows the products on October 13, you probably can't buy the LG gear this fall. The hub comes first, and the accessories follow.

## What Is Apple's Smart Home Hub?

The LG devices are described as part of a bigger package. Engadget lists a new hub, a revamped HomePod mini and a TV set-top box as related products. TechCrunch says the hub is reportedly scheduled for October 13 and describes a square display that can sit on a counter or mount on a wall.

Details vary by outlet. TechCrunch reports a 6-inch screen, while AppleWorld.Today, summarizing Gurman, reports a 7-inch display and a Siri-driven AI interface that "looks like a mashup of iOS and watchOS." Treat the size as unconfirmed until Apple speaks.

## Why Is Apple Partnering With LG?

Neither company has explained, so this is analysis, not reporting. The two firms already work together. According to Engadget, LG supplies the panels for Apple's Studio Display lineup, and LG smart TVs support Apple software.

Building smart home gear is a different business from building displays. Locks, doorbells and cameras mean ongoing support, firmware updates and hardware returns. Letting an established appliance maker carry that load, under its own brand, would let Apple enter the category without running it day to day.

The report also fits a broader picture: Apple has trailed Amazon and Google in smart home devices for years. A hub plus partner-built accessories would be one way to catch up.

## FAQ

### Are Apple and LG really making smart home devices together?

According to a Bloomberg report by Mark Gurman, yes, but neither company has confirmed it. The reported lineup includes a smart lock, a video doorbell, a thermostat and indoor and outdoor cameras. Engadget says the report draws on LG regulatory filings. Treat it as a credible rumor until Apple or LG announces it.

### Will the devices say Apple or LG?

LG, reportedly. Engadget says the products will be sold under the LG banner, even though Apple helped develop them. LG would also handle manufacturing and customer support. That would make this a partnership product line rather than an Apple-branded one, so don't expect an Apple logo on the doorbell.

### When can I buy the Apple and LG smart lock and doorbell?

Not soon. An announcement is expected as soon as October 13, 2026, but Engadget reports the LG accessories won't reach the market for at least a few more months. No US price has been reported, so budget flexibly and wait for official information.

### What is Apple's smart home hub?

It's a rumored device with a square display that can stand on a counter or mount on a wall. TechCrunch reports it's scheduled for October 13 with a 6-inch screen, while AppleWorld.Today reports a 7-inch screen. Details vary, so wait for Apple's official announcement.

### Does the FCC approve smart home devices?

In the US, the FCC authorizes devices that emit radio signals before they can be sold. Reports of this partnership cite regulatory filings, which often reveal product names and photos early. A filing shows a product is being prepared, but it does not confirm a launch date or price.
