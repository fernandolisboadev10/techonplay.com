OpenAI’s Sora is gone: the app closed on April 26 and the API was scheduled to shut down on September 24, so the **best AI video generators** for beginners now come from Google, Kuaishou, Runway, MiniMax, and ByteDance.

Alibaba’s Wan 3.0 now tops the Artificial Analysis text-to-video leaderboard, with MiniMax H3 and ByteDance’s Seedance 2.5 close behind and Google’s Gemini Omni Flash 1.1 in 10th. Kling keeps the most useful free tier. Any ranking written in spring, when Sora and Veo set the pace, is out of date.

This guide ranks six tools you can use today. You get the current version of each, what it costs, whether a free option exists, and who should skip it. Prices were last checked on October 26, 2026 where noted; the leaderboard figures were re-read the same day.

## What Changed in AI Video Since Sora Left

Six dates reshaped the field:

📅 **April 26:** OpenAI closes the Sora web and app experiences 📅 **May 19:** Google announces Gemini Omni Flash at I/O 📅 **June 17:** Kling 3.0 Turbo launches with faster, cheaper clips 📅 **July 31:** MiniMax H3 and ByteDance’s Seedance 2.5 arrive 📅 **August 24:** Alibaba launches Wan3.0 after a public beta that began August 6 📅 **September 24:** The Sora API was scheduled to sunset

The [Artificial Analysis text-to-video leaderboard](https://artificialanalysis.ai/video/leaderboard/text-to-video) shows the shift. As of October 26, Wan 3.0 is first at 1174 Elo, followed by Utopai X (1168), MiniMax H3 at 768p (1158), HeyGen Video 1 (1157) and Dreamina Seedance 2.5 (1156). Gemini Omni Flash 1.1 is 10th at 1128. Kling 3.0 Omni (Pro, 1080p) scores 1020 and Veo 3.1 scores 961. Runway does not appear in the top 15. The board has been re-scored since our September version, when Omni Flash led at 1233, so compare ranks rather than old Elo numbers.

📊 Treat those scores as a guide, not a verdict. They measure blind votes across many prompts, not your workflow or your budget.

## 1\. Gemini Omni Flash: Edit Videos by Chatting

Gemini Omni Flash is the easiest way to start, even though it no longer leads the rankings. You describe a clip, watch it render, then tell the model what to change. Google says every instruction builds on the last, so characters stay consistent and the physics hold up. That beats re-rolling a whole video because one detail went wrong.

It accepts text, images, audio, and video as inputs and returns clips with synchronized audio. Google announced it on May 19, and clips run about 10 seconds. An August 27 update, Omni 1.1 Flash, reportedly chains clips up to 40 seconds through scene extension.

✅ Ranks 10th on the leaderboard (Omni Flash 1.1, 1128 Elo) ✅ Conversational editing with no re-rendering from scratch ✅ Free inside YouTube Shorts and the YouTube Create app ❌ Every output carries an invisible SynthID watermark you cannot switch off ❌ Google held back speech and audio editing at launch 💰 Google AI Plus $4.99/month (200 Flow credits), AI Pro $19.99/month (1,000 credits), AI Ultra from $99.99/month

## 2\. Kling 3.0: The Best Value and the Best Free Start

Kling 3.0 launched in February and remains the smartest budget pick. The June 17 Turbo version adds faster renders and better lip-sync, which makes talking-head and dialogue clips look far more natural. Turbo runs 3 to 15 seconds at up to 1080p, with audio included in the per-second price.

The free plan gives you 66 daily credits, but free clips carry a watermark that cannot be removed. Paid plans remove it and add commercial rights. Credits reset monthly with no rollover.

✅ Free daily credits let you test before paying ✅ Strong human motion and lip-sync ✅ Turbo API pricing around $0.11 per second at 720p and $0.14 at 1080p ❌ Kling 3.0 Pro scores 1000 Elo and Kling 3.0 Omni 1020, outside the top 15 ❌ Subscription credits expire at the end of each billing month 💰 Free, Standard $10/month (660 credits), Pro $37 (3,000), Premier $92 (8,000), Ultra $180 (26,000). Annual billing saves about 34%. \[Plan data from June 2026; Kling’s pricing page did not show plan figures when we rechecked on October 26, so confirm current prices.\]

## 3\. Runway Gen-4.5: The Pick for Camera Control

Runway is the tool for people who care about how a shot is built. Its control stack lets you direct the camera, not just describe a scene, and that suits filmmakers and editors more than casual posters.

The catch is cost. Gen-4.5 burns 12 credits per second. The Standard plan’s 625 credits buy about 52 seconds of Gen-4.5 per month, and Pro’s 2,250 credits buy about 187 seconds. The free plan gives 125 one-time credits and excludes Gen-4.5 entirely.

✅ Professional camera direction tools ✅ Cheaper Gen-4 Turbo stretches credits to about 125 seconds on Standard ❌ Priced around $0.23 per second of Gen-4.5, the highest here ❌ No Gen-4.5 on the free plan 💰 Standard $12/month, Pro $28, Max $76 (all billed annually). \[Monthly billing costs more: $15, $35 and $95 per month, checked October 26.\]

## 4\. MiniMax H3: Native 2K at a Low Price

MiniMax H3, also called Hailuo 3.0, launched July 31 and ranks third on the leaderboard at 768p (1158 Elo), with H3 Max seventh at 1152. It generates up to 2K video with native stereo audio and accepts text, image, video, and audio inputs. Clips run up to 15 seconds.

The pitch is price. MiniMax says H3 costs less than a third of mainstream models per second at 2K. API pricing lists $0.13 per second at 2K and $0.08 at 768p. You can also pick H3 inside the Hailuo web app. MiniMax announced an open-weights release too, though that matters mostly to developers.

✅ Third and seventh on the leaderboard with native stereo audio ✅ Low per-second pricing at 2K ❌ Hailuo plan pricing varies by region and account, so check it before paying ❌ 15-second clip cap 💰 API $0.08 to $0.13 per second

## 5\. Seedance 2.5: Long Clips With 50 References

Seedance 2.5 from ByteDance targets creators who need a longer, more controlled piece. Dreamina says it generates clips up to 30 seconds, supports 4K, and accepts up to 50 references across images, video, audio, and text. You can also edit specific timestamps or characters inside a finished clip.

Seedance 2.5 now ranks fifth on the leaderboard at 1156 Elo. Availability is still the problem. CapCut’s July 31 announcement said Dreamina would roll it out to subscriber accounts for users over 16 in Europe, Asia, the Middle East, and South America, and did not list the United States. We found no later US announcement, so check your region before subscribing. On price, Dreamina says its annual Advanced plan works out to about $0.097 per second at 720p, roughly $2.91 for a 30-second clip.

✅ 30-second clips, 4K, and 50 references ✅ Timestamp-level editing ❌ Regional rollout may exclude US users ❌ Free credit limits are not published in detail 💰 About $0.097 per second on the annual Advanced plan (720p)

## 6\. Veo 3.1: Cinematic Realism in Google Flow

Veo 3.1 is no longer a leaderboard contender. It sits at 961 Elo (21st), and Google now pairs it with Omni in the same studio. Veo still earns a spot for cinematic output up to 4K and for people already working inside Google Flow.

You reach it through Flow or the API. API pricing runs from $0.03 per second for Veo 3.1 Lite to $0.40 for the top Quality tier, with audio and 4K adding a premium. College students can get Google AI Pro free for one year.

✅ Polished realism and 4K output ✅ Lite tier keeps API costs low ❌ Ranks 21st on the leaderboard ❌ Quality tier gets expensive fast 💰 Included with Google AI Pro at $19.99/month, or API from $0.03 to $0.40 per second

## What Happened to Sora, and Where to Move

OpenAI ended the Sora web and app experiences on April 26, 2026, and the API was scheduled to end on September 24, 2026. Export your content at sora.chatgpt.com/sunset, because OpenAI says it will permanently delete Sora data afterward. Purchased credits stay usable for other OpenAI services like Codex.

Do not start a new project on Sora. Move chat-style editing to Gemini Omni Flash, budget work to Kling, and camera-heavy work to Runway.

## Two Models to Watch: Wan 3.0 and Grok Imagine 1.5

Alibaba launched Wan3.0 on August 24. It generates clips up to 30 seconds, accepts documents such as PDF, PPT, and Markdown as input, and now ranks first on the leaderboard at 1174 Elo. Some listicles call it open-weight. It is not: as of August 26, trackers found no weights, no license, and API-only access. Alibaba also ran a 30% API discount on selected platforms through September 23.

xAI’s Grok Imagine 1.5 turns a still image into a video clip of up to 720p. It is in API preview, and the launch page lists no pricing, so it is a developer tool for now. It ranks 12th on the leaderboard at 1057 Elo.

## FAQ

### What is the best AI video generator in 2026?

_Wan 3.0 leads the Artificial Analysis text-to-video leaderboard as of October 2026, but Gemini Omni Flash is the easiest to use because you edit clips by chatting. Kling 3.0 wins on value, and Runway Gen-4.5 wins on camera control. Your best pick depends on budget and how much editing you plan to do._

### Is there a free AI video generator?

_Yes. Gemini Omni Flash is free inside YouTube Shorts and the YouTube Create app, producing short clips. Kling gives 66 daily credits, though free clips carry a watermark. Runway’s free plan offers 125 one-time credits and excludes Gen-4.5, so it works best as a quick test._

### What happened to Sora?

_OpenAI closed the Sora web and app experiences on April 26, 2026, and the Sora API was scheduled to end on September 24, 2026. You can export your content at sora.chatgpt.com/sunset. Purchased credits stay usable for other OpenAI services such as Codex, so new projects should use another tool._

### Which AI video generator is cheapest to start with?

_Google AI Plus costs $4.99 per month and includes Gemini Omni Flash with 200 Flow credits. Kling’s Standard plan costs $10 per month for 660 credits, and Runway’s Standard plan starts at $12 per month billed annually. For zero cost, use the free tiers above._

### Can AI video generators create sound?

_Yes. Gemini Omni Flash returns clips with synchronized audio, Kling 3.0 Turbo includes audio in its per-second price, and MiniMax H3 generates native stereo audio. Google held back speech and audio editing at launch, so changing a voice after generation is limited on Omni for now._

## The Bottom Line: Start Free, Then Pay for One Tool

The best AI video generators in October 2026 no longer include Sora, and no single tool wins every job. Start with Gemini Omni Flash in YouTube Shorts or Kling’s free credits, then commit to one paid plan once you know what you make most.
